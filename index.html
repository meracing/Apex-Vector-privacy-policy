Privacy Policy for APEX VECTOR

App Name: APEX VECTOR (2D Kinetic Wall-Grind Vector Racer)  |  Package ID: com.apexvector.game  |  Developer: ME Racing (meracing)

1. Overview & Privacy-First Commitment

APEX VECTOR ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") is a 2D kinetic vector racing game developed by ME Racing. We respect your privacy and built APEX VECTOR with a strict privacy-first, offline-capable architecture.

  • No Account Required: We do not require you to register, log in, or provide any personal identity.
  • Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data, name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or GPS location.
  • 100% On-Device Progress: All campaign progress, lap records, ghost replays, and settings are stored strictly on your device.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

APEX VECTOR saves gameplay state locally on your device using Android internal app storage (files/apex_vector_save_v1.json) and local WebView storage (localStorage) solely so your progress persists between sessions:

  • Campaign & Level Progress: Unlocked sectors, completed tracks, medals earned (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Apex), and Apex Shards collected.
  • Personal Best Times & Ghost Replays: Your fastest lap times, checkpoint splits, and 60Hz ghost ship trajectory coordinates so you can race against your own Personal Best ghost.
  • Game Settings: Independent Music and SFX volume levels, selected ship hull, neon exhaust trail skin, and control loadout preferences.

How to Delete Your Local Data: You have complete control over your local data. You can reset individual track records inside the in-game Briefing → Reset PB button, or erase all saved data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling APEX VECTOR from your Android device (Settings → Apps → APEX VECTOR → Storage → Clear Data).

3. Device Permissions We Use and Why

APEX VECTOR requests zero dangerous runtime permissions. It uses only standard Android normal permissions required for core gameplay:

Android Permission Purpose in APEX VECTOR
android.permission.VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic vibration feedback during wall-grinding, boost pads, and hazard collisions.
android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used strictly for peer-to-peer 2-Device Local Wi-Fi Multiplayer packets on your local network (LAN) and optional Google Play In-App Purchases.
android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Displays your device's local LAN IP address (e.g., 192.168.x.x) in the 2-Device Wi-Fi Lobby so a second device on the same Wi-Fi router can connect.
com.android.vending.billing Enables optional Google Play In-App Purchases (60-Track DLC Expansion Pack and Remove Ads VIP Edition).

4. Local Wi-Fi Multiplayer (Peer-to-Peer LAN)

APEX VECTOR includes an optional 2-Device Local Wi-Fi Multiplayer mode (1v1 Duel Series and Co-Op Campaign Discovery). When two devices on the same Wi-Fi network play together, they exchange temporary UDP packets (port 41234) directly across your local Wi-Fi router. These packets contain only live race telemetry (room code, selected track ID, ship coordinates, and finish times). No multiplayer traffic is sent to external servers, and no chat or personal data is transmitted.

5. In-App Purchases & Third-Party Services (Google Play)

If you download APEX VECTOR from the Google Play Store and make an optional In-App Purchase (such as the 60-Track DLC Pack or Remove Ads VIP Edition), the transaction is processed securely by Google Play Billing. We never access or store your credit card, bank account, or billing details; we receive only a non-personal confirmation token from Google Play indicating which content SKU is unlocked.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

APEX VECTOR is an arcade vector racing game suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). Because the Game collects no personal data from any user, no personal data from children is collected, stored, or shared.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA / CPRA)

Because ME Racing does not collect, store, or sell personal information on external servers:

  • Access & Deletion: All gameplay records reside solely on your device and can be deleted at any time via Android system settings or by uninstalling the app.
  • No Sale or Sharing: We do not sell or share personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).
